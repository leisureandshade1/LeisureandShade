Outdoor dining, made considered

Make your outdoor space a natural extension of your home

An outdoor dining table can turn a terrace, balcony or garden into one of the most welcoming places in your home. In Dubai, where outdoor living can be enjoyed for much of the year, the right table should balance design, everyday comfort and practical performance.

Before choosing a style, think about how you plan to use the space. Is it for relaxed family meals, weekend entertaining or quiet coffee outdoors? Your answer will help guide the table size, shape, material and overall look.

Start with your lifestyle.

The best choice is not simply the largest or most striking table. It is the one that fits your space, supports the way you gather and feels right alongside the rest of your home.

1. Consider Dubai’s outdoor conditions

Outdoor furniture in Dubai needs to be selected with sunshine, heat, dust and occasional moisture in mind. Look for materials and finishes designed for outdoor use, and check the care guidance before you buy. Where possible, position the dining area under a pergola, umbrella or other suitable shade to make it more comfortable during brighter hours.

2. Choose materials that suit your style

The material has a major influence on the character and upkeep of your dining area. Stone-inspired finishes can create a refined, architectural feel, while timber-look surfaces bring warmth. Metal frames can give a clean, contemporary profile, and carefully selected outdoor-grade materials can make regular maintenance easier.

Travertine is loved for its natural variation and soft, elegant appearance. If you are drawn to this look, explore the Travertino Series for inspiration. Always review the specific product details to understand its construction, finish and recommended care.

Design tip: Repeat one finish or tone from your indoor space outside to create a smooth visual connection between the two areas.

3. Get the size and shape right

Measure the available area before selecting a table. Remember to leave enough room for chairs to move in and out and for people to walk around comfortably. A rectangular table can work well for longer terraces and larger gatherings. A round table can make a compact setting feel sociable, while a square table may suit a more intimate corner.

Use your measurements to compare product dimensions rather than relying on appearance alone. If you regularly host guests, consider how many people you need to seat on an ordinary day and on special occasions.

4. Think about chairs and everyday comfort

A dining table should work as part of a complete setting. Check that the height pairs comfortably with your chosen chairs, and consider armrests, seat cushions and the space needed between places. If the table will be used for long meals, comfort matters just as much as the visual design.

5. Create a cohesive outdoor look

Let the architecture and surroundings guide your palette. Neutral shades, tactile finishes and a few carefully chosen accessories can create a calm, timeless atmosphere. Add outdoor lighting, planters or a textured rug where appropriate, but leave enough open space for the dining area to feel relaxed rather than crowded.

6. Check maintenance before you decide

Ask how the table should be cleaned, whether it needs seasonal protection and which products should be avoided. Use covers where suitable, wipe away spills promptly and follow the manufacturer’s instructions. A little regular care can help keep an outdoor dining area looking inviting.

Find a table that feels right for your home

The perfect outdoor dining table brings together proportion, material, comfort and personal style. Take time to measure your space, compare finishes and think about the moments you want to create around it. With a considered choice, your outdoor area can become a welcoming setting for everyday meals and memorable gatherings alike.
